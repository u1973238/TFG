# Final project checklist

When every box is ticked, the project is finished. Each objective has a test, so you know when it's done.

## Game

- [ ] **1. Texts without mistakes.** All dialogues, missions and menus pass the Softcatalà checker with 0 errors, in one language (Catalan).
- [ ] **2. Smooth movement.** In a 2-minute recording, the spider never jerks, never drops off walls or ceilings by itself, and the camera never jumps.
- [ ] **3. The web looks like a thread.** A thin rope when swinging and grappling; bridges made of strands, not a flat band.
- [ ] **4. Nothing important is frozen.** The spider is animated while it moves (legs, or at least the body), and every NPC has an idle animation and reacts when you talk to it.
- [ ] **5. Sound everywhere.** Every player action has a sound, and there is background music and nature ambience.
- [ ] **6. A clean, consistent UI.** Main menu, pause, options, controls and victory screen all share one style, and all of them work with mouse and controller.
- [ ] **7. A real open world.** At least 4 different zones, each with a landmark, that can be visited in any order.
- [ ] **8. Real puzzles.** At least 3 puzzles that mix two or more mechanics. Test players solve them without help, but not on the first try.
- [ ] **9. Complete and stable.** All missions can be finished in any order, there's no way to get stuck, and the build runs at a steady 60 FPS.

## Delivery

- [ ] **10. Tested by others.** 3–5 people play it and fill in a short questionnaire, and the main problems they find are fixed.
- [ ] **11. Final build and demo video.** A working .exe and a 2–3 minute video showing every mechanic and puzzle.
- [ ] **12. Memòria updated.** It describes what the game really does, includes the playtest results and the new features, and passes the spell checker.
